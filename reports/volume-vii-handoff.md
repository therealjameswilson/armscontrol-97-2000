This page treats Volume VIII as the second half of the Clinton arms-control story. Each 1997-2000 lane should begin with the question left open by the 1993-1996 assister.

## Operating Rule

Public statements identify the handoff dates. Clinton Library, State FOIA, Congress.gov, NATO/OSCE, DOD/DOE, ACDA/State, and NARA source paths determine whether the continuity is strong enough for candidate document selection.
