# Clinton Library Research Plan

The target on-site workflow is to move from public anchors and FOIA source copies into internal NSC, State, Defense, ACDA, Legal Advisor, and Records Management files for 1997-2000.

## Priority Pulls

| Priority | Lane | Pull target | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | START/ABM | START Helsinki/START III Guidelines; START III/ABM PC Meeting; START III 2000 | Internal record behind Helsinki, Cologne, and Moscow public statements. |
| A | ABM/NMD | NMD/ABM 2000; PRD-31 Ballistic Missile Defense & ABM Treaty | Core chapter for NMD Act, ABM MOU, and Moscow summit diplomacy. |
| A | CTBT | Congressional Ratification of the Comprehensive Test Ban Treaty, 2015-1095-F | Main path into Senate ratification strategy and October 1999 defeat. |
| A | South Asia | 2006-0859-M; South Asia DC/PC and trip-book folders | Needed for India/Pakistan nuclear tests and strategic restraint diplomacy. |
| A | DPRK | DPRK/ROK collection, North Korea folder item 72959, Perry process, Taepo Dong, KEDO, and Albright-trip files | Needed so regional proliferation covers the late-Clinton Korea missile/nuclear arc, not only South Asia. |
| A | CFE | CFE-Istanbul Summit Documents; CFE-October/November Endgame | Conventional arms-control endpoint for 1999 adaptation. |
| B | CBW | Elisa Harris CWC files; CWC/BWC legal and implementation files; Helms treaty files | Turns public treaty statements into implementation, compliance, and Senate-side records. |
| B | Fissile/CTR | Plutonium, early-warning, MPC&A, and June 2000 Russia/Ukraine trip files | Internal basis for public plutonium and early-warning statements. |

## Capture Standard

For each folder, record exact folder title, OA/ID, source part/page, date span, restriction/declassification markings, item titles, attachments, and page ranges. Public speeches and speechwriting folders should be used to backtrace clearance chains after policy files are screened.
